# Startup Finance & Fundraising Curriculum

**Role level:** 40 (deep functional pillar — Startup family)
**Family:** Startup
**Status:** planned — modules and projects below are the planned scope authored from [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json). Lessons and worked exemplars will be drafted by subsequent autonomous content cycles, oldest-gap first.

## Overview

The deep functional pillar for the **capital branch** of the startup dependency graph — Startup Accounting Foundations → Unit Economics & Cohort Financial Modeling → Three-Statement Model & Driver-Based Forecasting → Cap Tables & Equity Compensation → Convertible Instruments → Startup Valuation → Fundraising Strategy & Investor Targeting → Term Sheets & Preferred-Stock Economics → Runway Management & Bridge / Alternative Financing → Board & Investor Governance for the CFO → Finance Operations, Controls & Team Design. Covers IDEA-stage founder-math through the pre-IPO CFO seat.

The eleven modules move a finance leader from a founder-bookkeeping cash-basis P&L (*what is a startup's actual revenue this month?*), through cohort-based unit economics rigorous enough to defend a Series-A round (*what is the discount-rate-adjusted LTV of a customer, per channel, at CFO grade?*), through a driver-based three-statement model reconciled monthly (*does a single assumption change ripple through the P&L, balance sheet, and cash-flow statement without breaking?*), through the cap-table and equity-comp mechanics that determine founder dilution across a decade (*what happens to the founder stake at Series-B under a 1x participating stack?*), through the convertible instruments that fund seed and bridge rounds (*what does the cap table look like after five stacked YC post-money SAFEs convert?*), through valuation across stages (*Berkus at angel, VC method at Series-A, Meritech-anchored revenue multiples at Series-B*), through running a raise end-to-end (*investor targeting, funnel management, deck architecture, data room, close choreography*), through negotiating a term sheet against the NVCA reference (*liquidation preferences, anti-dilution, protective provisions, board composition*), through runway and bridge capital (*extension SAFEs, venture debt, revenue-based financing, Reg A+*), through the CFO's board and investor governance work (*monthly updates, quarterly board packs, decision memos, board-meeting design, CFO-CEO partnership*), and finally through the finance-org design that scales from founder-bookkeeping through the pre-IPO CFO team (*stack selection by stage, first-ten-hires staging, month-end close, audit-readiness, SOX-lite controls, IPO dual-track*). Total planned commitment: **256 hours across 11 modules** + **96 hours across 3 projects** = **~352 hours**.

## Ownership rule

Following the project-wide ownership rule, this curriculum:

- **Owns** the capital-branch depth from IDEA-stage founder-math through the pre-IPO CFO seat — Startup Accounting Foundations, Unit Economics & Cohort Financial Modeling, Three-Statement Model & Driver-Based Forecasting, Cap Tables & Equity Compensation, Convertible Instruments, Startup Valuation Frameworks, Fundraising Strategy & Investor Targeting, Term Sheets & Preferred-Stock Economics, Runway Management & Bridge / Alternative Financing, Board & Investor Governance for the CFO, and Finance Operations / Controls / Team Design.
- **Defers down** to [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) (level 10) for the founder-numbers slice — runway, burn, growth read, default alive / default dead, North-Star metric selection. This track *never* re-teaches those; it layers CFO-grade cohort-modelling and capital-structure depth on top.
- **Defers sideways** to [`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum) for the GTM-operator view of CAC / LTV / payback / magic number (this track owns the cohort-based, discount-rate-adjusted, board-defensible modelling of those same numbers and the fundraising-narrative packaging); to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) for equity-compensation *policy* (this track owns equity *economics* — dilution math, option-pool math, waterfall analysis, 409A methodology, tax preference mechanics); to [`startup-exit-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-exit-curriculum) for M&A / IPO / secondary-transaction / shutdown *transaction* mechanics (this track owns the ongoing-company financial infrastructure and captable that feeds those transactions and the CFO-side dual-track audit-readiness pre-work).
- **Defers sideways / up** to the role-pathway repos ([`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum), [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum), [`cto-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum)) that inherit this curriculum at their declared coverage and add role-specific curated views — no fork, no duplication.

Below this pillar sits a future `startup-accounting-controllership` track (not yet in the ladder) that would own bookkeeping / month-end / audit-mechanics depth in the way an assistant controller or staff accountant would experience it — this pillar covers those at the CFO-decision level (which stack to buy, when to hire, when to audit) rather than the operator-mechanic level.

## Module summaries

### mod-101 — Startup Accounting Foundations
Read and produce a startup's three-statement financials on both cash and accrual basis; apply ASC 606's five-step revenue-recognition model to SaaS contracts (annual prepay unwinding monthly out of deferred revenue); reconcile the four SaaS numbers a CFO reports separately (bookings, billings, revenue, cash) that a founder frequently conflates; recognise gross-vs.-net revenue and contra-revenue; diagnose the cash-to-accrual transition triggers. Grounded in FASB ASC 606, IRS Publication 538 on cash vs. accrual, the AICPA FRF for SMEs, and the practitioner content library from Kruze Consulting.

### mod-102 — Unit Economics & Cohort Financial Modeling
Model unit economics at CFO grade — fully-loaded CAC per channel per cohort, cohort-based LTV with a defensible discount rate (not blended ARR × 1/churn), payback period in months from a real cohort table, gross-margin bridge decomposed to hosting / support / delivery, NRR / GRR walk as compounding capital-efficiency levers, and the modern capital-efficiency instruments (burn multiple, cash-conversion-score, Rule of 40) benchmarked against OpenView / SaaStr / a16z. Recognises the sideways boundary with the GTM-operator view owned by `startup-product-gtm-curriculum`.

### mod-103 — Three-Statement Model & Driver-Based Forecasting
Build a driver-based three-statement (P&L + balance sheet + cash-flow) startup financial model from scratch — assumptions / drivers / hiring plan / GTM funnel / P&L / balance sheet / cash-flow / KPI dashboard — reconciled monthly across all three statements. Author top-down and bottom-up forecasts; author scenario / sensitivity analysis with two-variable heatmaps for the levers that dominate cash-out date; produce a live board-ready KPI dashboard reconciled to the model. Diagnose common model failures (hard-coded overrides, circular refs, orphaned tabs, opening-balance breaks) and know when to graduate off spreadsheets onto Causal / Runway / Mosaic / Anaplan / Adaptive.

### mod-104 — Cap Tables & Equity Compensation
Model a startup cap table end-to-end — issued vs. outstanding vs. fully-diluted; do pre-money vs. post-money math correctly including the option-pool 'shuffle'; size ESOP top-ups per round; author liquidation-waterfall analyses under different exit prices and preference stacks; apply 409A methodology and Rule 701 aggregate-value caps; design the equity-comp toolkit (ISO / NSO / RSU, cliff / vesting, 83(b), early exercise, QSBS, secondary tender). Recognises the sideways boundary — equity-comp *policy* defers to `startup-operations-governance-curriculum`; this module owns equity *economics*.

### mod-105 — Convertible Instruments
Structure a fundraise using YC's current post-money SAFE (four variants), recognise the pre-money legacy SAFE and its different cap-table impact, structure convertible notes with maturity mechanics, stack multiple convertibles on a cap table and model the pro-forma conversion at a priced Series-A. Author side letters and MFN amendments; apply Reg D (Rules 504, 506(b), 506(c)) exemptions correctly; diagnose common failure modes (SAFE overhang, mixed pre-/post-money stacks, notes converting at maturity).

### mod-106 — Startup Valuation Frameworks
Apply pre-revenue anchor methods (Berkus, Payne Scorecard, risk-factor summation) at angel / pre-seed; apply the VC method at seed / Series-A (target return → target exit value → back-solve pre-money); apply revenue-multiple valuation at Series-A and later anchored to Meritech public comps / Bessemer Cloud Index and growth-adjusted (Rule of 40); recognise where DCF applies and where it fails for startups; read Fenwick / WSGR / PitchBook-NVCA / Carta State of Private Markets as the quarterly market-conditions reference. Grounded in Damodaran's academic-practitioner work and Kupor's inside-the-VC-firm perspective.

### mod-107 — Fundraising Strategy & Investor Targeting
Run a raise end-to-end — size the round correctly against milestone bar rather than backing into a dilution target; construct an investor-target list against thesis / stage / cheque size / partnership economics; design a fundraising funnel with weekly tracking; author a pitch deck in the Sequoia / YC pattern with DocSend-tracked iteration; construct a data room organised for VC diligence; choreograph the close across parallel processes to converge term sheets in a window; understand the CFO's role in the raise (model authorship, term-sheet analysis, choreography) alongside the CEO's narrative-and-relationship half. Grounded in Feld & Mendelson, Kupor, YC, Sequoia, DocSend, and the operator-blog canon (Wilson, Suster).

### mod-108 — Term Sheets & Preferred-Stock Economics
Read a term sheet against the NVCA reference — market-standard vs. aggressive vs. unusual clauses. Negotiate liquidation preferences (1x non-participating vs. 1x participating vs. capped participation, senior vs. pari-passu stacks) with worked waterfall math across exit scenarios; anti-dilution recalculation (broad-based weighted-average, narrow-based, full ratchet); pro-rata / super-pro-rata / lead-pro-rata; protective provisions; board composition and independent-director selection; drag-along / tag-along / redemption / MFN. Grounded in the quarterly deal-terms data (Fenwick, WSGR, Carta) as the market-conditions reference.

### mod-109 — Runway Management & Bridge Financing
Manage runway with 18-24 month planning discipline layered on top of the founder-numbers slice from Foundations; structure bridge rounds (extension of existing preferred, insider-led bridge SAFE, bridge convertible note); manage down rounds with pay-to-play mechanics, recapitalisations, senior-preference stacking, common-employee re-up grants; structure venture debt (SVB / TriplePoint / Runway / Hercules pattern) and revenue-based financing (Pipe / Capchase / Founderpath / Arc); understand growth-stage capital instruments (ATM, PIPE, Reg A+, 144A). Recognises the sideways boundary — M&A / IPO / secondary *transactions* defer to `startup-exit-curriculum`.

### mod-110 — Board & Investor Governance for the CFO
Author monthly investor updates in the concise voice a busy investor will read; author quarterly board packs (KPI dashboard reconciled to the model, financials, strategic deep-dive, risks / decisions / consents) delivered 72 hours pre-meeting; design board meetings (pre-read → KPI check-in → strategic deep-dive → decisions → executive session); author board consents, decision memos, and running action-item registers. Design board committees (audit, comp, transaction) with charters, cadence, and independent-director requirements. Manage the CFO-CEO partnership across the fundraising cycle and difficult board conversations with honest-signal delivery.

### mod-111 — Finance Operations, Controls & Team Design
Choose the finance-ops stack by stage (Mercury / Brex / Ramp on ops, QBO / Xero / NetSuite on ledger, Gusto / Rippling on payroll, Airbase / Ramp on AP, Coupa / Blackline / Adaptive at growth); sequence the finance function's first ten hires against stage triggers and Carta / Pave comp benchmarks; design the month-end close on a 5-business-day close target; design audit-readiness for the first outsourced audit (typically Series-B); design SOX-lite controls at Series-B; manage R&D tax credits and sales-tax nexus; design IPO-readiness / dual-track pre-work. Recognises the sideways boundary — full IPO / M&A *transaction* execution defers to `startup-exit-curriculum`; controller / staff-accountant depth defers to a future `startup-accounting-controllership` track.

## Assessment

Each module ships **1 quiz** plus the exercises and labs listed in [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json). Each project ships a portfolio-grade README under [`projects/`](projects/), a filled exemplar under [`exemplars/`](exemplars/) (three-statement financial model with scenarios and sensitivity, cohort unit-economics pack, complete cap table with stacked SAFEs and Series-A/B priced rounds under multiple preference stacks, convertible-instrument conversion memo, valuation memo across methods, pitch deck with DocSend-tracked iteration, data-room layout, term-sheet red-line with negotiation memo, runway / capital-strategy memo, monthly investor updates plus quarterly board pack, finance-org and controls plan), and an explicit rubric covering three-statement-model reconcilability, unit-economics defensibility, cap-table correctness, waterfall analysis accuracy, valuation-method fit to stage, pitch-deck-market-fit signal read, term-sheet negotiation against the NVCA reference, runway-scenario discipline, board-pack quality against a real board's expectations, and finance-org staging defensibility.
