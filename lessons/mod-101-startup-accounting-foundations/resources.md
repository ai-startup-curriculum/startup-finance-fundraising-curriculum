# Resources — mod-101 Startup Accounting Foundations

This is the reference stack that sits behind every chapter and exercise in this module. Links are grouped by tier (see chapter 7). Read the tier-1 primary sources first; treat tier-4 practitioner content as interpretive, not authoritative.

## Tier 1 — Authoritative US GAAP (FASB)

- **FASB Accounting Standards Codification** — the single source of authoritative US GAAP. Basic view is free; register at [asc.fasb.org](https://asc.fasb.org/). Full access requires a subscription; libraries and CPA firms typically hold subscriptions.
- **FASB ASC Topic 606 — *Revenue from Contracts with Customers*** — the five-step model (chapter 2). Includes implementation guidance sections (55-XX paragraphs) and industry-specific examples.
- **FASB ASC Topic 340-40 — *Other Assets and Deferred Costs — Contracts with Customers*** — capitalisation and amortisation of contract-acquisition costs (sales commissions) and contract-fulfilment costs.
- **FASB ASC Topic 230 — *Statement of Cash Flows*** — presentation and classification rules for the cash-flow statement.
- **FASB ASU 2014-09** — the original standard introducing ASC 606, jointly issued with IFRS 15. Subsequently amended by several ASUs (2015-14 deferral, 2016-08 principal-vs.-agent clarification, 2016-10 licensing and identifying performance obligations, 2016-12 narrow-scope improvements, 2016-20 technical corrections).
- **FASB Emerging Issues Task Force (EITF)** archive — [www.fasb.org/eitf](https://www.fasb.org/eitf) — historical positions on emerging accounting issues.

## Tier 2 — Authoritative tax guidance (IRC, Treasury, IRS)

- **Internal Revenue Code (26 U.S.C.)** — statutory federal-tax law. Full text at Cornell's LII: [law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/26).
  - IRC §446 — general rule on methods of accounting.
  - IRC §448 — limitation on use of cash method (the C-corporation / partnership restriction and the small-business exception).
  - IRC §451 — taxable year of inclusion of income.
  - IRC §461 — taxable year of deduction of expenses.
  - IRC §471 — general rule on inventories, with the §471(c) small-business exception.
  - IRC §263A — uniform capitalisation of inventory costs (UNICAP).
  - IRC §481(a) — adjustments required by changes in methods of accounting.
- **Treasury Regulations §1.446-1 and following** — governing detail on methods of accounting.
- **IRS Publication 538 — *Accounting Periods and Methods*** — [irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p538.pdf](https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/p538.pdf). Plain-English practitioner guide to cash vs. accrual, the hybrid method, restrictions, and the mechanics of changing accounting methods.
- **Form 3115 — *Application for Change in Accounting Method*** — [irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-3115](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-3115). Filed when a taxpayer changes accounting methods.
- **IRS Revenue Procedures for automatic accounting-method changes** — currently in a series that began with Rev. Proc. 2015-13 and is updated periodically. Check for the current-year version before filing.
- **IRS webpage on accounting periods and methods** — [irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/accounting-periods-and-methods](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/accounting-periods-and-methods).

## Tier 3 — Non-GAAP special-purpose frameworks

- **AICPA Financial Reporting Framework for Small- and Medium-Sized Entities (FRF for SMEs)** — [aicpa-cima.com](https://www.aicpa-cima.com/) → search "FRF for SMEs." Alternative to full US GAAP for private companies; modified accrual with simpler revenue-recognition and no consolidation of VIEs.
- **AICPA Audit and Accounting Guides** — subscription access via aicpa-cima.com. Relevant guides for this module include *Revenue Recognition* and industry-specific guides for technology / software.

## Tier 4 — Startup-specific practitioner content

Practitioner content is interpretive, not authoritative. Useful for translating standards into workflow and for benchmarking practices, but every position should be cross-referenced to a primary source.

- **Kruze Consulting** — [kruzeconsulting.com/blog](https://kruzeconsulting.com/blog) — outsourced-CPA firm serving early-stage startups; content covers startup tax, R&D tax credits, revenue recognition, month-end close, audit prep, and 409A refresh.
- **Kruze *Startup CFO Financial Terms* glossary** — [kruzeconsulting.com/blog/startup-financial-terms/](https://kruzeconsulting.com/blog/startup-financial-terms/) — plain-language definitions of the terms in this module.
- **Pilot** — [pilot.com/blog](https://pilot.com/blog) — outsourced-bookkeeping and outsourced-CFO service; content on ASC 606 for SaaS, deferred revenue, month-end close, and finance-ops stack.
- **Bench** — [bench.co/blog](https://bench.co/blog) — cash-basis-first outsourced bookkeeping; useful for the pre-seed cash-basis window.

## Tier 5 — Adjacent references useful for context

### IFRS (for international operations)

- **IFRS 15 — *Revenue from Contracts with Customers*** — the IASB standard converged with ASC 606. Standard summary at [ifrs.org](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ifrs-15-revenue-from-contracts-with-customers/).

### Big Four / second-tier practitioner interpretive guides

- **Deloitte — *A Roadmap to Applying the New Revenue Recognition Standard*** — [deloitte.com](https://www.deloitte.com/) → Insights → Accounting.
- **PwC — *Revenue from contracts with customers* guide** — [viewpoint.pwc.com](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/) (free registration).
- **EY — *Financial reporting developments — Revenue from contracts with customers*** — [ey.com](https://www.ey.com/) → Insights → Accounting.
- **KPMG — *Handbook: Revenue recognition*** — [frv.kpmg.us](https://frv.kpmg.us/).
- **RSM, BDO, Grant Thornton** publish similar interpretive guides for the mid-market segment.

### SaaS metrics and unit economics (background for chapter 3)

- **David Skok — *SaaS Metrics 2.0*** — [forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/](https://www.forentrepreneurs.com/saas-metrics-2/). The canonical SaaS-metrics essay covering bookings, MRR, ARR, churn, CAC, LTV. Foundational reading before mod-102.
- **a16z — *16 More Startup Metrics*** — [a16z.com/16-more-metrics-to-watch/](https://a16z.com/16-more-metrics-to-watch/). Definitions of ARR, MRR, gross vs. net revenue, GMV vs. revenue, bookings vs. billings vs. revenue.
- **a16z — *16 Startup Metrics*** — [a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/](https://a16z.com/16-startup-metrics/). Companion piece.

### SEC (post-IPO context)

- **SEC EDGAR** — [sec.gov/edgar](https://www.sec.gov/edgar). Access to public-company filings; read the "Revenue" note in any recent SaaS 10-K for real-world revenue-recognition disclosure examples.
- **SEC Financial Reporting Manual** — [sec.gov/corpfin/cf-manual/](https://www.sec.gov/corpfin/cf-manual/).

## Reading order for this module

If you have never read the primary standards, this is the order to read them:

1. IRS Publication 538 (chapter 6 background).
2. FASB ASC 606-10-05 (introduction and objective — 5 paragraphs).
3. FASB ASC 606-10-25 (recognition — steps 1, 2, and 5 of the model).
4. FASB ASC 606-10-32 (measurement — steps 3 and 4).
5. FASB ASC 606-10-55 (implementation guidance — especially 55-36 to 55-40 on principal vs. agent).
6. FASB ASC 606-10-45 (presentation — contract asset / contract liability).
7. FASB ASC 606-10-50 (disclosure — including the roll-forward and RPO).
8. FASB ASC 340-40 (contract-acquisition and fulfilment costs).
9. FASB ASC 230 (cash-flow statement).
10. AICPA FRF for SMEs — skim if you're at Series-A or earlier without a GAAP audit in the near future.
11. A Big Four revenue guide (any of Deloitte / PwC / EY / KPMG) — read the sections most relevant to your specific product.
12. A public-SaaS 10-K revenue-recognition footnote — pick a company in your space.

Only after those should you rely on practitioner blog content, and even then, read it as a translation layer rather than a source of truth.
