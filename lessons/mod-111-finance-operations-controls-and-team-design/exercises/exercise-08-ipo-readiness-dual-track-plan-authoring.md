# Exercise 08 — IPO-Readiness / Dual-Track Plan Authoring

**Estimated time:** ~5 hours
**Prerequisites:** All chapters (1-9), especially chapters 4 (audit), 5 (controls), 6 (tax), 7 (sales tax), and 8 (IPO-readiness). Exercises 04 (audit-readiness) and 05 (controls) are strongly recommended as inputs.

## Problem statement

Author the 18-month IPO-readiness plan for a specified Series-C SaaS company on a dual-track (IPO / M&A) trajectory. Deliverables: a T−18-to-T-day master timeline across audit, close, SOX-404, S-1 / MD&A, and dual-track workstreams; workstream-owner assignments and staffing plan; an auditor-transition memo (if applicable); a SOX-404 program design memo; an S-1 drafting-workstream calendar; an MD&A skeleton with two quarters of illustrative period-over-period commentary; a dual-track choreography memo; and a post-IPO CFO calendar-shape memo.

The goal is to produce the CFO's operating artifact for the single most-choreographed 18-24 months of the CFO's career. The pre-IPO CFO who cannot produce a plan of this shape is either about to slip the IPO by 6-12 months or about to hit an SEC comment-letter cycle that surfaces gaps the plan would have caught. The exercise is not the finished plan — every plan is company-specific — but the discipline of building one, naming the specific gating constraints, and choreographing the workstreams against a single calendar.

**Sideways-deferral reminder.** This exercise scopes the *operating-side* of IPO-readiness — audit, close, SOX, S-1 authoring from the company side, MD&A drafting, dual-track finance-team choreography. It does *not* scope the transaction-execution mechanics (underwriter selection deep-dive, pricing mechanics, roadshow choreography, SEC comment-letter response process, syndicate structure, lock-up mechanics). Those belong sideways to [`startup-exit-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-exit-curriculum). The plan you produce should acknowledge those handoffs explicitly without attempting to author them.

## Scenario — build your own

Construct (or use a real one) a Series-C SaaS company with the following characteristics:

- ~$120M-$180M ARR, ~600 employees, one US HQ entity plus one EU subsidiary and one UK subsidiary, growing 40-60% YoY.
- Cash: ~$200M on hand post-Series-C, ~$120M projected at IPO after 18-24 months of continued growth investment.
- Two-year audit history: first audit committed FY(N−3) (see exercise 04); FY(N−2) audit expected to complete Q1 of FY(N−1); FY(N−1) audit will complete Q1 of FY(N) — the intended IPO year.
- Auditor: currently one of the top-tier second-tier PCAOB-registered firms (BDO, Grant Thornton, RSM, or similar). The audit committee has discussed whether to switch to Big Four for the pre-IPO period; document your recommendation.
- Controls: SOX-lite program in place from Series-B (see exercise 05); ~80 controls in the matrix; no material weaknesses in the first two audits; ready to formalise as SOX-404.
- Close: 5-business-day month-end; 6-8 business-day quarter-end; NetSuite is the GL; FloQast or Blackline installed for close-task management. Public-company close discipline not yet installed.
- FP&A: Head of FP&A in role; Adaptive Planning platform; internal management reporting on the ARR / NRR / CAC / magic-number KPI set.
- IR: Head of IR hired 6 months ago from a public-company IR role.
- Legal: general counsel + a corporate finance outside-counsel relationship at a large national firm; NVCA-model documents in force from Series-A / B; corporate-governance rebuilt in the last 12 months to add independent directors and formal audit / comp / nom-and-gov committees (mod-110 chapter 6).
- JOBS Act status: the company qualifies as an Emerging Growth Company (EGC) — revenue below the current EGC threshold; verify the specific threshold as most recently indexed and cite in your plan.
- Target IPO fiscal year: FY(N), specifically a Q2 or Q3 pricing window.
- Dual-track: the board has directed the CFO / CEO to run a dual-track through T−6 to T−3 with a specific convergence-decision milestone.
- Reasonable variations are welcome; document any assumption changes upfront.

## Requirements

Produce the following artifacts.

### Artifact 1 — T−18-to-T-day master timeline (spreadsheet or Gantt-style visualisation, plus 3-4 page walk-through memo)

The master timeline mapping the five workstreams onto a single 18-24 month calendar. Workstreams and required milestones:

- **Audit workstream.** FY(N−2) audit start / fieldwork / opinion; FY(N−1) audit start / fieldwork / opinion; auditor-transition (if applicable); PCAOB-registration confirmation; independence letter cadence; audit-committee cadence.
- **Close workstream.** Public-company close rehearsal; earnings-release-pack draft; 10-Q / 10-K template installation; investor-facing metric definitions locked; close-calendar compressed to public-company timeline.
- **SOX-404 workstream.** Scoping; process narratives per in-scope process; ITGCs deployed; entity-level controls documented; test-of-design; test-of-operating-effectiveness; deficiency evaluation; management assessment authored; §404(a) filed at year 1 post-IPO; §404(b) deferred per EGC (up to 5 years).
- **S-1 / MD&A workstream.** Bake-off; underwriter selection; S-1 drafts 1-6; MD&A drafts; confidential DRS filing; SEC comment-letter response cycles; public filing; pricing; roadshow.
- **Dual-track workstream.** Sell-side mandate on the private path; late-stage-round conversations if applicable; secondary-tender option; convergence decision at T−6 to T−3.

The timeline should show every milestone with month, owner, upstream dependency, downstream dependency, and any specific SEC / stock-exchange / PCAOB-imposed date constraint.

The 3-4 page memo walks the reader through the timeline: the specific gating constraint (the two-year audit requirement — chapter 8 explicit), the critical path through the SOX-404 program, the S-1 drafting cadence, and the specific decision-and-convergence moments in the dual-track.

### Artifact 2 — Workstream-owner and staffing plan (2-3 pages)

Per workstream:

- **Named owner** (finance function or cross-functional role).
- **Backup owner** (specifically who covers if the primary is unavailable).
- **Internal staffing** (headcount required across the 18 months; where net-new hires are needed vs. where existing staff scale into the role).
- **External advisor mix** (SOX advisor, S-1 counsel, financial printer, transfer agent, IR firm, banker mix). Cite specific firm-types by name where the CFO would name them; do not skip the advisor layer.
- **Hand-off points** — the specific handoffs between workstreams (audit → SOX; SOX → S-1 controls disclosure; MD&A → earnings release template; dual-track → definitive path selected).
- **Budget** — a top-of-page annual budget number across the 18 months for advisors, tooling, and internal-staffing incremental cost.

### Artifact 3 — Auditor-transition memo (2-3 pages)

The specific decision on whether to switch from the current second-tier PCAOB-registered firm to a Big Four for the pre-IPO period. Sections:

- **The case for switching.** Investor / underwriter perception, depth of SEC-registrant-audit experience, depth of technical-accounting resources, depth of SOX-404 audit experience.
- **The case for staying.** Institutional knowledge, cost, relationship continuity, no incremental fee premium.
- **The specific timing constraint.** A switch adds 3-6 months to the timeline and costs the incumbent's audit-history knowledge (chapter 8); if the switch is made, model when it must be made to preserve the IPO calendar.
- **Recommendation** with an explicit trade-off statement. Include the specific pre-approval workflow for the new firm's non-audit services (if any).

If the company stays with the incumbent, produce an incumbent-firm expansion memo — the specific incremental scope the incumbent needs to add for pre-IPO readiness (SOX-404 audit team, SEC-registrant reporting team, MD&A review, comfort-letter mechanics).

### Artifact 4 — SOX-404 program design memo (4-6 pages)

The specific formalisation of SOX-lite (from exercise 05) into full SOX-404. Sections:

- **Scoping.** Significant accounts and disclosures list; in-scope business processes; in-scope entities (including the EU and UK subsidiaries); in-scope IT systems (NetSuite, billing, payroll, equity administration, sales-tax vendor).
- **Entity-level controls.** Board oversight, code of conduct, whistleblower program, financial-reporting risk assessment, tone-at-the-top — with the specific evidence produced.
- **Process narratives.** For each in-scope process (order-to-cash, procure-to-pay, payroll, financial-close, treasury, tax, stock-based-comp, IT change-management), a target date for the walkthrough document and the specific process-owner.
- **ITGCs and application controls.** The specific IT-general-controls (access, change management, computer operations, program development) and application controls per system.
- **Test-of-design and test-of-operating-effectiveness.** The specific cadence, sample sizes, and testing-window for each control category.
- **Deficiency-evaluation framework.** The specific rubric (deficiency / significant deficiency / material weakness) with the escalation path.
- **Management assessment.** The §404(a) assessment authoring process, timing, and the audit-committee readout.
- **Staffing.** The internal audit team hire (2-6 people), the external SOX advisor engagement (Big Four or top second-tier consulting practice — distinct from the audit firm), and the CFO ownership. Include the specific hire sequence and target dates.

### Artifact 5 — S-1 drafting workstream calendar (2-3 pages)

The specific drafting-workstream calendar for the 18-month window. Sections:

- **Kickoff and section-owner assignments.** Every S-1 section from chapter 8 with a named primary author (CFO / CEO / general counsel / underwriter counsel / auditor) and the review process for each.
- **The 4-6 formal drafts.** Target date for each draft; the working-group (company, underwriters, underwriter counsel, company counsel, auditor); the "drafting session" cadence (weekly progressing to daily as pricing approaches).
- **Confidential DRS filing.** Target month; the specific JOBS Act EGC confidentiality mechanic; the SEC-review kickoff.
- **SEC comment-letter response cycles.** Target 2-4 rounds over 6-12 weeks; the specific process for tracking, drafting responses, and coordinating with auditor and counsel.
- **Mid-cycle financial-statement update.** If a quarter closes during the SEC-review window, the specific update process.
- **Financial printer engagement.** Target month; the specific vendor selection.
- **Effective date and pricing.** Target month; the pre-pricing-week freeze.

### Artifact 6 — MD&A skeleton with two quarters of illustrative period-over-period commentary (3-4 pages)

The specific MD&A section skeleton, following chapter 8's reference structure, filled in with:

- **Overview and key metrics.** Company description, key KPIs (ARR, NRR, customer count, magic number, etc. — pick the specific set that fits the scenario). Every KPI defined with its calculation.
- **Components of results.** Revenue, cost of revenue, operating expenses, non-operating items, income taxes — each defined and characterised.
- **Two quarters of period-over-period commentary.** Author two illustrative quarters showing the specific *quantified-driver* discipline chapter 8 describes — every variance broken down into the driver components with dollar amounts. Choose realistic (illustrative — mark `[assumption]`) dollar movements.
- **Liquidity and capital resources.** Cash sources and uses, working capital, contractual obligations. Illustrative but structurally complete.
- **Critical accounting policies and estimates.** The specific positions where management judgment is significant — revenue recognition (ASC 606), stock-based comp (Black-Scholes / Monte Carlo assumptions), income taxes (deferred tax valuation, R&D-credit-related uncertain tax positions), goodwill / intangibles impairment (if any past M&A).

The two quarters of commentary should demonstrate the *style* of MD&A writing that clears SEC comment — direct, quantified, driver-attributed, period-consistent. This is the artifact that most directly signals the CFO's readiness for public-company reporting.

### Artifact 7 — Dual-track choreography memo (3-4 pages)

The specific plan for running the dual-track process. Sections:

- **The parallel private paths in scope.** Which combination of M&A (sell-side bank mandate), late-stage private round (crossover investor conversations), or secondary tender the company is running in parallel. State the specific rationale for each path being in scope.
- **The private-path calendar.** Bank selection, management-presentation deck, buyer / investor list, indication-of-interest / non-binding-LOI milestones, definitive-agreement diligence. Overlay onto the master timeline.
- **The IPO-path calendar.** The T−18-to-T-day calendar from artifact 1.
- **The convergence decision.** The specific decision milestone (T−6 to T−3), the specific decision-makers (board, CFO, CEO), the specific decision criteria (relative valuation, timing certainty, operating-model fit, stakeholder mix, market conditions per chapter 8).
- **The cost model.** Advisor cost for the parallel tracks (banker retainer + success fee + counsel fees + PR / IR firm fees); the incremental finance-team staffing cost; the CFO / CEO / general counsel calendar burden.
- **Cross-contamination controls.** The specific process for keeping the M&A information tent and the IPO information tent separated — bidder-side confidentiality mechanics, IPO-side selective-disclosure discipline, the specific personnel who work only in one tent vs. across both.
- **The wind-down plan for the non-selected path.** Once the convergence decision is made, the specific communication to the non-selected side (bankers, buyers, investors) and the specific wind-down of the working-group cost.

### Artifact 8 — Post-IPO CFO calendar-shape memo (1-2 pages)

The specific memo the CFO would use to prepare for the post-IPO year. Sections:

- **The calendar shape shift.** From the pre-IPO ~50-60% operating and ~40-50% deals split to the ~25% operating and ~50% market-facing and ~25% regulatory split.
- **The quarterly-earnings cycle.** The specific cadence — earnings release, earnings call, 10-Q filing, guidance update, buy-side / sell-side follow-ups.
- **The IR calendar.** Analyst-day, conference attendance (~8-12 per year), 1:1 investor meetings.
- **The board / audit-committee cadence.** Monthly audit-committee for the first year, quarterly board, annual meeting, proxy prep.
- **The ongoing SOX / SEC / insider-trading regime.** The specific compliance overhead.
- **The CFO-fit self-assessment.** Chapter 8 raises the specific question of whether the pre-IPO CFO is the public-company CFO. Author a specific self-assessment against the calendar shape and the specific criteria the company would use if a public-company CFO succession were being considered.

## Starter guidance

- **Build the master timeline first.** Every other artifact hangs off it. If your timeline has a gap or a broken dependency, the rest of the artifacts inherit the problem.
- The two-year audit requirement (chapter 8) is the *hard gating constraint*. Every other workstream can be compressed or expanded; the audit history cannot be moved. Author your plan working backward from the FY(N−1) audit-opinion date.
- The JOBS Act EGC status (chapter 8) meaningfully changes the SOX-404 §404(b) attestation timeline (deferred up to 5 years post-IPO) and the S-1 financial-statement depth (two years of audited operations sufficient rather than three). Verify the current EGC revenue threshold and other qualification criteria; tag with `[assumption]` if unverified.
- The MD&A drafting is the specific place where CFOs who delegate get SEC-comment-heavy first drafts. Author your two illustrative quarters yourself; do not templatise.
- Use exercises 04 (audit-readiness), 05 (controls), 06 (R&D), and 07 (sales tax) as *inputs* — the pre-IPO plan is the integration of the streams the earlier exercises exercised individually.
- Cross-reference mod-110 chapter 6 (audit / comp / nom-and-gov committees) — the pre-IPO governance rebuild is a specific input to the IPO-readiness plan, not a separate workstream.
- Cross-reference mod-107 (fundraise) and mod-109 (runway) — the dual-track late-stage private path is the mod-107 raise workstream running in parallel; the runway math (mod-109) drives the specific "when must we price / close" backstop against the dual-track convergence decision.
- The `startup-exit-curriculum` deferral is explicit — the transaction execution (pricing mechanics, roadshow, SEC-comment-letter response process at the specific-tactical level, syndicate structure) is out of scope. Your plan acknowledges the handoff without attempting the transaction execution itself.
- Any regulatory citation (EGC thresholds, SEC filing dates, PCAOB rules) you cannot verify to the current-year source should be tagged `[assumption]` or `<!-- needs-research: ... -->` per the module convention.

## Acceptance criteria

- **The master timeline spans T−18 to T-day** with every workstream mapped onto it and every dependency named.
- **The two-year audit gating constraint drives the timeline** and is called out explicitly in the walk-through memo.
- **The workstream-owner-and-staffing plan names specific owners, backup owners, and external advisors** with a target budget and headcount plan.
- **The auditor-transition memo makes a specific recommendation** with a trade-off statement and timing analysis.
- **The SOX-404 program-design memo covers all seven design components** (scoping, entity-level, process narratives, ITGCs and app controls, tests of design and operating effectiveness, deficiency evaluation, management assessment).
- **The S-1 workstream calendar names section owners and target drafts** with the DRS filing and SEC comment-letter cycle called out.
- **The MD&A skeleton includes two quarters of quantified-driver period-over-period commentary** in the chapter-8 style.
- **The dual-track memo names the specific parallel paths, the convergence-decision milestone, and the cross-contamination controls.**
- **The post-IPO calendar memo includes the CFO-fit self-assessment.**
- **Every unverified regulatory or timing claim is tagged** `[assumption]` or `<!-- needs-research: ... -->`.

## Extensions (optional)

- **Model a market-closed scenario.** The IPO window closes at T−3 (macro-driven; the specific IPO market closes for 6-12 months). Author the specific plan for continuing IPO-readiness while the window is closed and the specific criteria for reopening the process.
- **Model a material-weakness scenario.** The FY(N−1) audit surfaces a material weakness in a specific area (revenue recognition, stock-based comp, or an ITGC gap). Author the remediation-and-disclosure plan and the impact on the IPO calendar.
- **Model an M&A convergence.** The dual-track converges on an M&A outcome rather than the IPO. Author the specific hand-off of the finance-ops artifacts to the acquirer's post-close integration team, and the specific communication to the working-group on the IPO side.
- **Model a spin-off / carve-out.** The company is contemplating spinning off a business unit as part of the IPO or as an M&A carve-out. Author the specific carve-out financial-statements workstream, the additional audit-and-controls scope, and the impact on the master timeline.
- **Cross-track integration with `startup-exit-curriculum`.** For the transaction-execution mechanics deliberately out of scope in this module, author a specific hand-off memo — the specific artifacts the CFO delivers to the transaction-execution workstream, the specific coordination points, and the specific division of labour across the T−3-to-T-day window.
