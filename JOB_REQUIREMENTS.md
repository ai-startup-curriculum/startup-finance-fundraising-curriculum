## Status — bootstrap session, live postings deferred

Startup Finance & Fundraising covers the **capital branch** of the startup dependency graph — Startup Accounting → Unit Economics & Cohort Financial Modeling → Three-Statement Model & Driver-Based Forecasting → Cap Tables & Equity Compensation → Convertible Instruments → Startup Valuation → Fundraising Strategy → Term Sheets & Preferred-Stock Economics → Runway Management & Bridge / Alternative Financing → Board & Investor Governance for the CFO → Finance Operations, Controls & Team Design — from IDEA-stage founder-math through the pre-IPO CFO seat. Unlike a single-title role, the equivalent live-market postings span several hireable titles and multiple company stages. The live cycle should backfill from:

- **Founder / CEO adjacency:** Founder / CEO postings that explicitly name owning fundraising and capital allocation at pre-seed → seed. Filter to postings where the founder is the finance owner (there is no CFO yet).
- **Fractional / interim finance titles:** *Fractional CFO*, *Interim CFO*, *Outsourced Controller-With-CFO-Duties* at pre-Series-A startups. Boards: Kruze Consulting, Burkland, Pilot, Bench, and independent-CFO marketplaces.
- **Founding-team finance titles:** *Founding Finance Lead*, *Founding VP Finance*, *Head of Finance (founding)* — seed → Series-A, usually <30 headcount, where the hire owns the first CFO seat.
- **Head-of-Finance / VP-Finance titles:** *VP Finance*, *Head of Finance*, *Director of Finance* at Series-A → Series-B companies (30-150 headcount).
- **CFO titles:** Full *CFO* at Series-A → pre-IPO growth-stage companies. Executive-search postings: Heidrick, Spencer Stuart, True Search, Daversa, plus venture-firm portfolio-jobs boards (a16z, Sequoia, Bessemer, Accel, GV, Founders Fund, USV, Redpoint, Kleiner Perkins).
- **Strategic-finance specialist titles:** *Head of Strategic Finance*, *Head of FP&A*, *Director of FP&A* at Series-B → growth-stage companies.
- **Investor-relations titles:** *Head of Investor Relations* at pre-IPO issuers and newly-public companies.

Postings for **pure controller / assistant-controller / staff-accountant / bookkeeping titles that do not include fundraising, capital-allocation, or board-facing responsibility** should defer *down* to a future `startup-accounting-controllership` track — those cover the mechanical side of the finance stack but not the CFO-grade depth this pillar owns.

## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical CFO / finance-leader task domains at the *pillar* level from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json) `authoritative_references` — the term-sheet + fundraising canon (Feld & Mendelson, Kupor, YC, Sequoia), the NVCA reference documents, the quarterly deal-terms data (Fenwick, WSGR, Carta, PitchBook-NVCA, CB Insights), the cap-table + equity-comp canon (Carta, Holloway, IRC 409A / 83(b) / 1202, Rule 701), the accounting canon (ASC 606, AICPA), the unit-economics canon (Skok, OpenView, Bessemer, Meritech, a16z, SaaStr), the valuation canon (Damodaran, Berkus / Payne), the governance canon (Feld, Wilson, Gurley, Suster, First Round), and the securities-law framework (SEC Reg D, Rule 701, Reg A+, Form S-1).
2. Anchored the taxonomy against the three-axis model authored in [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) — the [dependency graph](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md) (capital branch owned by this repo), the [stage ladder](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md) (IDEA → PRE-SEED → SEED → SERIES-A → GROWTH → MATURE), and the founder-numbers slice (never re-taught here).
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — this repo owns the capital-branch depth from IDEA-stage founder-math through the pre-IPO CFO seat. Everything else defers:
   - *Down* to [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) (level 10) for the founder-numbers slice (runway, burn, growth read, default alive / default dead, North-Star metric selection). This track *never* re-teaches those; it layers CFO-grade modelling and capital-structure depth on top.
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum) for the GTM-operator view of CAC / LTV / payback / magic number (this track owns the cohort-based, discount-rate-adjusted, board-defensible modelling of the same numbers and the fundraising-narrative packaging).
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) for equity-compensation *policy* (this track owns equity *economics* — dilution math, option-pool math, waterfall analysis, 409A methodology, tax preference mechanics).
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-exit-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-exit-curriculum) for M&A / IPO / secondary-transaction / shutdown *transaction* mechanics (this track owns the ongoing-company financial infrastructure and captable that feeds those transactions, and the CFO-side dual-track audit-readiness pre-work).
   - *Up* to [`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum), [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum), and [`cto-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum) — role-pathway repos that reference this curriculum rather than re-authoring it.
4. Sized each module against the level-40 depth-and-leadership target (~340–360h total across modules + projects), stage-tagged per [`STARTUP_STAGES.md`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md) — mod-101 → IDEA / PRE-SEED, mod-102–103 → PRE-SEED / SEED, mod-104–108 → SEED / SERIES-A, mod-109 → SERIES-A / SERIES-B, mod-110 → SERIES-A / B / C, mod-111 → SERIES-B / C.
5. Flagged every theme's frequency as `needs-research` so the first live research cycle can populate the number without restructuring the catalog.

## Ownership map — quick reference for the next cycle

When backfilling postings, use this ownership decision to keep this pillar from drifting into Foundations, GTM, operations-governance, or exit-transaction territory:

- **Startup Finance & Fundraising** (this repo, level 40) — owns Startup Accounting Foundations, Unit Economics & Cohort Financial Modeling, Three-Statement Model & Driver-Based Forecasting, Cap Tables & Equity Compensation, Convertible Instruments, Startup Valuation Frameworks, Fundraising Strategy & Investor Targeting, Term Sheets & Preferred-Stock Economics, Runway Management & Bridge / Alternative Financing, Board & Investor Governance for the CFO, and Finance Operations / Controls / Team Design. IDEA-stage founder-math through the pre-IPO CFO seat.
- **Startup Foundations** (`startup-foundations`, level 10) — the *system*, the *graph*, the *stages*, the founder's operating loop, and the founder-numbers slice (runway / burn / growth). Prerequisite. *Never* re-taught here.
- **Startup Product & GTM** (`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`, level 30) — owns the customer-facing branch (Discovery, PMF, Positioning, Founder-Led Sales, Pricing, Motion, Demand Gen, Retention, GTM Metrics & First Hires). Sideways — this pillar covers the *operator* view of CAC / LTV / payback / magic number as GTM instruments; this repo covers the cohort-based, discount-rate-adjusted, board-defensible modelling and the fundraising-narrative packaging.
- **Startup Operations & Governance** (`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`, level 25) — people, legal, operations, governance. Sideways — this pillar owns equity-comp *policy* (grants, refresh, promotion, comp-committee); this repo owns equity *economics* (dilution, option-pool, waterfall, 409A, tax preference).
- **Startup Exit & Endgame** (`startup-exit-curriculum`, level 40) — M&A, secondaries, IPO, succession, shutdown *transactions*. Sideways — this repo produces the ongoing-company financial infrastructure and captable that feed those transactions; that track owns transaction execution mechanics.
- **Founder / CEO** (`founder-ceo-curriculum`, level 30) — CEO-level fundraising, term-sheet negotiation, board-partnership, and capital-allocation framing. Sideways role-pathway peer — references this curriculum at ~70% coverage for the CEO seat.
- **CPO** (`cpo-curriculum`, level 30) and **CTO** (`cto-curriculum`, level 30) — role pathways that reference this curriculum for the unit-economics / cohort-modelling / capital-planning depth their board-partnership work requires, and add role-specific curated views.
