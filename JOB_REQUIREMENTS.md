This file documents the requirements catalog used to seed the Startup Finance & Fundraising curriculum. Raw normalised data lives in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json); the planned curriculum lives in [`.aicg/curriculum-plan.json`](.aicg/curriculum-plan.json).

## Status — second live cycle, 51 postings sampled, zero curriculum additions proposed

Startup Finance & Fundraising covers the **capital branch** of the startup dependency graph — Startup Accounting → Unit Economics & Cohort Financial Modeling → Three-Statement Model & Driver-Based Forecasting → Cap Tables & Equity Compensation → Convertible Instruments → Startup Valuation → Fundraising Strategy → Term Sheets & Preferred-Stock Economics → Runway Management & Bridge / Alternative Financing → Board & Investor Governance for the CFO → Finance Operations, Controls & Team Design — from IDEA-stage founder-math through the pre-IPO CFO seat. The equivalent live-market postings span several hireable titles and multiple company stages. This cycle sampled 51 in-window postings across those equivalent titles:

- **Fractional / Interim CFO** at pre-Series-A startups — 11 postings (6 retained — QuantFi, One Stop Swap, Secure Engage, Ascent CFO Solutions, premarket medical-device startup, a SaaS-for-accounting-firms company; 5 fresh — Zeni, Product.ai, SuperSummary, Pink Elephant, Practice Flow; Wells Group, Equanimo, TAINA aged out of window; Kindwell Partners observed but excluded because the posting body 404'd on direct fetch).
- **Founding Finance Lead / VP Finance / Head of Finance / Director of Finance** at seed → Series-B — 20 postings (12 retained — Assort Health, Juno, Evonaire, Tiny Health, Hone Health, Burq, AirGarage, Noma Security, Traba, Braintrust, Hyperbolic Labs, Capsa AI; 8 fresh — Freshpaint, Midstream Health, bunch, Pubity Group, Compa, GC AI, Nightfall AI, Odys Aviation).
- **Full CFO** at Series-A → pre-IPO / growth-stage — 10 postings (9 retained — Scratch Financial, a VC-backed biotech via Harmonic Finance, a clinical-stage biotech via Meet Life Sciences, a Series-B Web3 fintech via Hays, Certa, a fast-growth B2B SaaS via Harmonic Finance, a B2B SaaS/cybersecurity via Jobgether, Halo Investing, Kalohexis appointment signal; 1 fresh — Emergent Labs. A confidential UK Series D pre-IPO SaaS CFO via Vertex Talent was observed but kept out of the counted sample because the employer could not be verified to a canonical URL).
- **Head of Strategic Finance / FP&A** at Series-B → growth-stage — 8 postings (6 retained — Numeral, Rox, Capchase, Factory, the Harmonic Series-C fintech, the Harmonic Series-C biotech; 2 fresh — Deep Infra Director FP&A, Fireworks AI Strategic Finance Manager).
- **Head of Investor Relations** at pre-IPO / newly-public issuers — 2 postings (Fivetran, Plaid; the IR market remains thinly posted and executive-search listings are frequently blind — no new IR postings cleared the audit bar this cycle).

Postings for **pure controller / assistant-controller / staff-accountant / bookkeeping titles that do not include fundraising, capital-allocation, or board-facing responsibility** were filtered out — those defer *down* to a future `startup-accounting-controllership` track. **Founder / CEO postings** where the founder is the finance owner were also filtered out this cycle because that scope is authoritatively owned by [`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum).

## Methodology

1. Sourced the canonical CFO / finance-leader task domains at the *pillar* level from the public references catalogued in [`.aicg/job-requirements.json`](.aicg/job-requirements.json) `authoritative_references` — the term-sheet + fundraising canon (Feld & Mendelson, Kupor, YC, Sequoia), the NVCA reference documents, the quarterly deal-terms data (Fenwick, WSGR, Carta, PitchBook-NVCA, CB Insights), the cap-table + equity-comp canon (Carta, Holloway, IRC 409A / 83(b) / 1202, Rule 701), the accounting canon (ASC 606, AICPA), the unit-economics canon (Skok, OpenView, Bessemer, Meritech, a16z, SaaStr), the valuation canon (Damodaran, Berkus / Payne), the governance canon (Feld, Wilson, Gurley, Suster, First Round), and the securities-law framework (SEC Reg D, Rule 701, Reg A+, Form S-1).
2. Anchored the taxonomy against the three-axis model authored in [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) — the [dependency graph](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/FUNCTIONAL_CURRICULA.md) (capital branch owned by this repo), the [stage ladder](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md) (IDEA → PRE-SEED → SEED → SERIES-A → GROWTH → MATURE), and the founder-numbers slice (never re-taught here).
3. Applied the **ownership rule** — this repo owns the capital-branch depth from IDEA-stage founder-math through the pre-IPO CFO seat. Everything else defers:
   - *Down* to [`startup-foundations`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations) (level 10) for the founder-numbers slice (runway, burn, growth read, default alive / default dead, North-Star metric selection). This track *never* re-teaches those; it layers CFO-grade modelling and capital-structure depth on top.
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-product-gtm-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-product-gtm-curriculum) for the GTM-operator view of CAC / LTV / payback / magic number (this track owns the cohort-based, discount-rate-adjusted, board-defensible modelling of the same numbers and the fundraising-narrative packaging).
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-operations-governance-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-operations-governance-curriculum) for equity-compensation *policy* (this track owns equity *economics* — dilution math, option-pool math, waterfall analysis, 409A methodology, tax preference mechanics).
   - *Sideways* to [`startup-exit-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-exit-curriculum) for M&A / IPO / secondary-transaction / shutdown *transaction* mechanics (this track owns the ongoing-company financial infrastructure and captable that feeds those transactions, and the CFO-side dual-track audit-readiness pre-work).
   - *Up* to [`founder-ceo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/founder-ceo-curriculum), [`cpo-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cpo-curriculum), and [`cto-curriculum`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/cto-curriculum) — role-pathway repos that reference this curriculum rather than re-authoring it.
4. Sized each module against the level-40 depth-and-leadership target (~340–360h total across modules + projects), stage-tagged per [`STARTUP_STAGES.md`](https://github.com/ai-startup-curriculum/startup-foundations/blob/main/STARTUP_STAGES.md) — mod-101 → IDEA / PRE-SEED, mod-102–103 → PRE-SEED / SEED, mod-104–108 → SEED / SERIES-A, mod-109 → SERIES-A / SERIES-B, mod-110 → SERIES-A / B / C, mod-111 → SERIES-B / C.
5. Applied the continuity bias from the research packet — default to no change; add only when ≥3 postings AND ≥0.30 frequency AND no existing module/exercise can be incrementally extended. All three emerging themes near the threshold this cycle (emerging-03 agentic-finance tooling at 0.27 blended, emerging-01 international multi-entity at 0.25, emerging-02 IPO-readiness at 0.20) have a pre-committed extension path into an existing module (mod-111) rather than a new module.

### How to read the verbatim frequencies

Themes 4, 5, 6, and 8 (cap-tables, convertibles, valuation, term sheets) continue to show low or zero verbatim frequency in the sample. This is **not** a signal to reduce coverage. At CFO / VP-Finance / Head-of-Finance altitude, employers assume this knowledge and foreground the execution work (running the raise, closing the round, defending the model) rather than enumerating mechanics. The evidence remains unchanged from the prior cycle: postings that quote *fundraising support*, *investor materials*, *data room*, *diligence*, and *Series B readiness* (now 41/51 = 80%) are pricing-in that the hire will bring cap-table, convertible, valuation, and term-sheet mechanics with them. Preserve full coverage of mod-104, mod-105, mod-106, mod-108 and treat low verbatim frequency as an artifact of high-altitude JD prose, not as a curriculum-trimming signal.
